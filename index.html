01 About me

Partnerships that turn national programs into real reach.

Aishya Fazahra is a strategic partnerships and program & campaign management professional at the Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs of Indonesia, where she has worked on the National Digital Literacy Program since February 2021.

She leads the planning, coordination, and delivery of large-scale national events and campaigns — connecting government officials, corporate partners, and industry stakeholders — and builds long-term relationships with 146+ multi-stakeholder partners across corporations, government institutions, NGOs, and media.

She also served as a Secretariat Member of the National Digital Literacy Movement (GNLD) Siberkreasi from 2022 to 2025, facilitating cross-sector collaboration across a network of 170+ stakeholders. Her background is in International Relations, with a concentration in International Political Economy.
